Before you come in

What should I wear?

Comfortable workout clothes. Bring water.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you and your goals. We'll talk it through together. You never have to commit to a package on your first visit.

Does it hurt?

Clients describe warmth, gentle pressure or rolling. Tell us any time you'd like the intensity changed.

Can I come with a friend?

Yes. We keep three Bodyshape, Vacutherm, Rollshape and infrared capsule machines, so up to three of you can book the same time. EM Sculpt and T-Shape are single machines, booked one client at a time.

How do packages work?

Buy a package and its sessions land in your account as credits. Each booking uses one automatically. Packages last 30 days, and a credit comes back if you cancel at least 24 hours ahead.

Is it right for everyone?

Not always. If you're pregnant, have a pacemaker or other implant, a heart condition, or recent surgery, please talk to us, and check with your doctor, before booking.
